# Social Media Image Sizes Cheat Sheet 2026

Use these dimensions as starting points for preparing social images. Display crops, upload limits, and available formats vary by placement and device; a profile display size is not necessarily an upload recommendation. Preview the result in the publishing app before posting.

For a single photo going to several channels, the [Social Media Image Pack](/social-media-image-pack) prepares feed, story, thumbnail, and pin crops together. Mark the subject once, adjust individual outputs, and download a ZIP.

## Instagram Image Sizes

| Format | Size | Aspect Ratio |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Square post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 |
| Portrait post | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 |
| Landscape post | 1080 × 566 | 1.91:1 |
| Story / Reels | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Profile picture | 320 × 320 | 1:1 |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350 | 1:1 or 4:5 |

**Tip:** A 4:5 crop leaves more vertical space than a square crop at the same width. Choose the framing that suits your subject; a particular aspect ratio does not guarantee higher engagement.

## YouTube Image Sizes

| Format | Size | Aspect Ratio |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Video thumbnail (current official recommendation) | 3840 × 2160 | 16:9 |
| Shorts thumbnail (current official recommendation) | 2160 × 3840 | 9:16 |
| Channel banner | 2560 × 1440 | 16:9 |
| Profile picture | 800 × 800 | 1:1 |
| Community post | 1200 × 675 | 16:9 |

YouTube's [custom thumbnail guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en), checked on September 9, 2026, recommends the resolutions above. Upload limits depend on the device and content type; consult that guidance before exporting a large file.

Our [YouTube Thumbnail Checker](/youtube-vertical-thumbnail-checker) currently exports a 1280 × 720 custom-thumbnail crop and a 1080 × 1350 composition preview. These are tool output sizes, not the latest recommended maximum-quality resolutions. Use [Custom Size Cropper](/crop-image-by-dimensions) for an exact pixel target. The checker compares framing; it does not reproduce YouTube's automatic thumbnail selection.

## Pinterest Image Sizes

| Format | Size | Aspect Ratio |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Standard pin | 1000 × 1500 | 2:3 |
| Square pin | 1000 × 1000 | 1:1 |
| Vertical video canvas | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Profile picture | 165 × 165 | 1:1 |

Pinterest's [creator FAQ](https://create.pinterest.com/faq/) recommends 2:3 images at 1000 × 1500 and 9:16 videos at 1080 × 1920. This is format guidance, not a promise of distribution. ImageCropKit crops still images; it does not export video.

## X / Twitter Image Sizes

| Format | Size | Aspect Ratio |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Single-image post (example canvas, not an upload requirement) | 1600 × 900 | 16:9 |
| Header (official recommendation) | 1500 × 500 | 3:1 |
| Profile picture (official recommendation) | 400 × 400 | 1:1 |

X's [profile upload guidance](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo) recommends the header and profile dimensions above and limits profile photo files to 2 MB. It also warns that headers can be cropped differently across browsers and monitor sizes. Keep important text away from the top and bottom edges and check the saved profile on desktop and mobile.

Use [exact-size cropping](/crop-image-by-dimensions) for the header and avatar. Multi-image post layouts and website preview cards are separate formats; do not treat the example post canvas as a universal requirement for them.
